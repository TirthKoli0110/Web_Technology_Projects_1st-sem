Basic Soccer Rules

  1. Each team normally has 11 players on the field.
  2. The match is played in two halves.
  3. Players must not deliberately handle the ball, except the goalkeeper in their permitted area.
  4. A goal is scored when the entire ball crosses the goal line between the goalposts and beneath the crossbar.
  5. Players must follow the referee's decisions.
  6. Fair play and sportsmanship are important.
